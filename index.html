PAYMENTS · BEGINNER GUIDE

Set up Alipay
step by step

Follow this guide before your trip. We'll help you register, add your international card, and check that you're ready to pay.

You'll need:
📱 Your phone · 🛂 Your passport · 💳 Your own bank card · 📩 Access to SMS verification
Step 1 of 6

Download the official Alipay app

Open the App Store or Google Play and search for Alipay. Check the app name and publisher before installing.

📱
SCREENSHOT NEEDEDApp Store / Google Play search result showing the official Alipay app
🐼 Panda Tip: Download the main Alipay app. Don't confuse it with AlipayHK unless you already use that wallet.
Step 2 of 6

Open Alipay and register

Open the app. If prompted, choose your country or region and enter the mobile number you can access while travelling.

📱
SCREENSHOT NEEDEDRegistration screen with country code and phone-number field
Use your own number. Keep access to it so you can receive verification messages.
Step 3 of 6

Verify your phone number

Enter the verification code sent by SMS. Follow the app's on-screen instructions to finish registration.

🔐
SCREENSHOT NEEDEDSMS verification-code screen. Do not show a real personal code.
Never share your verification code with anyone, including someone claiming to be customer support.
Step 4 of 6

Find Bank Cards

In Alipay, open Me (usually the bottom-right tab), then look for Bank Cards.

👤
SCREENSHOT NEEDEDAlipay “Me” page with the Bank Cards entry circled
App layout can change. If you don't see Bank Cards, use the app's search or help function.
Step 5 of 6

Add your international bank card

Tap Add Bank Card. Enter your card details as requested and follow any verification from your bank.

💳
SCREENSHOT NEEDEDAdd Bank Card screen with the button and card-entry area highlighted

Supported card networks and eligibility may vary. Check the current in-app list. Never upload or share your card number, expiry date, CVV or banking OTP.

Step 6 of 6

Check that your card is linked

Return to Bank Cards and check that your card appears. If the app requests identity verification, follow the prompts in the official app.

✅
SCREENSHOT NEEDEDBank Cards page showing a successfully added card — use a demo card or redact all sensitive details
🐼 You're nearly ready! Try a small payment at a participating merchant when you arrive. Keep cash or another payment method as backup.
Useful phrase: “Can I pay with Alipay?”
可以用支付宝吗？

Important: App screens, card eligibility, verification requirements, fees and limits may change. Follow the current instructions shown in Alipay and your card issuer's app.

A SIMPLE SETUP

Don't try to learn every app.

For a typical first visit, prepare payment, messaging, navigation, rides, bookings and translation. Add local apps only when you need them.

  1. Before departure: install, register and test payment and connectivity options.
  2. Save offline: hotel address in Chinese, booking confirmations, passport copy and emergency contacts.
  3. On arrival: connect, check payment, then navigate to your hotel.
Important: Google Maps, Gmail, WhatsApp, Instagram and other services may be inaccessible on ordinary mainland-China networks. A travel eSIM or roaming plan may change connectivity; don't assume a VPN or eSIM will work without checking its provider's current terms and testing it before departure.

Emergency Help & Survival Chinese 🆘

Save essential numbers and copy simple phrases to communicate when you need help.

110

Police · 报警

Crime, immediate danger or urgent police assistance.

119

Fire & rescue · 火警

Fire, rescue and other critical incidents.

120

Ambulance · 急救

Medical emergencies and ambulance assistance.

122

Traffic accident · 交通事故

Traffic police assistance for road accidents.

PHASE 4 · OFFICIAL WORKFLOW REVIEW

Foreign visitor booking workflows

Use your passport consistently: enter the passport details required by the booking channel and bring the original document used for the reservation. Requirements vary by venue.

Municipal parks: the official Visiting Beijing Parks platform supports passport bookings. First-time identity binding may require a mainland China mobile number. Participating parks may offer staffed ticket windows for visitors who cannot book online.

No WeChat or foreign number: check the venue's official English page first. For parks covered by the municipal guidance, ask about on-site staff-assisted ticket purchase. Do not assume walk-in entry is possible where advance reservation is mandatory.

Tian'anmen Square: the official FAQ says the web portal may not support international phone registration and recommends the WeChat mini-program. Visitors without WeChat may authorize someone else to reserve on their behalf.

Official-source review: 22 November 2026 · Confirm venue-specific rules before travel.

Reservation failure checklist

  • Check passport name and number carefully.
  • Confirm the attraction accepts foreign passports in this booking channel.
  • Check whether a mainland phone number is needed for identity binding or SMS.
  • Try another official channel listed by the attraction.
