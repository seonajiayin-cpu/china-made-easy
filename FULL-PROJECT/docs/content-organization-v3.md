# Content organization v3

## City attraction browsing
City modals now display attraction entries inside topic accordions. Topic groups are collapsed by default and searchable. Current groups:
- History & culture
- Nature & scenery
- City & neighbourhoods
- Modern landmarks
- Seasonal attractions
- Other attractions (fallback)

Classification is editorial and based on the attraction's primary visitor experience. Search matches English name, Chinese name, summary, and group label. It does not imply verified addresses, opening hours, ticketing, or geographic routing.

## Experience browsing
Experience cards are organized into collapsible groups based on the existing data category:
- Culture & traditions
- Food & dining
- Food experiences
- Food & markets
- Wellness
- Entertainment
- Social games
- Activities
- Other experiences

Categories are derived from `data/experiences.json`; existing records are preserved.
