# Phase 140 — Chengdu & Chongqing Summary Localization

## Scope
Localized existing English attraction summaries for six languages on ten attraction records in Chengdu and Chongqing.

## Records
- Chengdu Panda Base
- People's Park
- Wuhou Shrine & Jinli
- Kuanzhai Alleys
- Chengdu Museum
- Sanxingdui Museum
- Hongya Cave
- Jiefangbei
- Liziba
- Yangtze Cableway

## Editorial status
All newly written localized summaries are machine-assisted drafts derived from the existing English summary in the project. They are not represented as official translations and require editorial review.

## Verification boundary
No ticket prices, opening hours, booking URLs, addresses, official sources, or other externally verifiable facts were newly invented or changed in this phase.
