# Nested Content Organization

The main content hub is organized into six top-level categories. Each category contains collapsible subcategories so visitors can progressively reveal content instead of seeing long sections at once.

- Prepare: Start here; Apps & payments
- Discover: Cities & destinations; Experiences; Food & dining
- Plan: Build your itinerary; Your travel record
- On your trip: Safety & emergency help
- My China: My travel memories; Account & privacy
- Help: Frequently asked questions; Trust & content information; Feedback & suggestions

The implementation moves existing sections into nested details elements. It does not remove content.
