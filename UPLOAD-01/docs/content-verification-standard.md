# Content Verification Standard — China Made Easy

## Status labels
- **Official-source-reviewed**: a relevant official visitor-information page was reviewed and linked. This does not mean all dynamic details are guaranteed current.
- **Cross-checked**: a claim was checked against at least two credible sources.
- **Needs verification**: no sufficient source review has been completed.
- **Seasonal/dynamic**: hours, prices, ticketing, access, events, transport, closures, weather, booking rules or other details can change and must be checked close to travel.

## Minimum record fields
- destination and attraction
- review date (ISO YYYY-MM-DD)
- status label
- source name and URL
- source topics reviewed
- dynamic items to recheck before travel
- known limitations

## Editorial rule
Never imply that a source review guarantees future availability, opening, price, access or booking. Keep stable background descriptions separate from dynamic operational details. Prefer official sources for admission, operating hours, access and booking. Cite and date reviews.

## Pilot
The Beijing pilot currently covers thirteen attraction/venue records: Forbidden City/Palace Museum, Mutianyu Great Wall, Temple of Heaven, Summer Palace, Jingshan Park, Beijing Zoo, National Museum of China, Yonghe Temple, Badaling Great Wall, Universal Beijing Resort, 798·751 Zone, National Stadium & National Aquatics Center, and Confucius Temple & Imperial College. It also includes one general official guide to municipal park ticketing for foreign visitors, for fourteen records total. The record is in `content/audits/beijing-pilot.json`.
