# Food & Dining Organization v1

Food cards are organized into expandable regional groups: Cantonese food, Sichuan & Chongqing, Northeast China, Northern China, and Street food & drinks. Existing region filters continue to work and automatically hide empty groups.

Restaurant directory entries are grouped by destination in expandable city groups. Existing city filters and text search continue to work; search opens groups containing matches. Restaurant data remains editorial research leads and is not represented as live verified availability or opening status.
