# Phase 40 — Travel Logistics Layer

## Scope
- Add logistics prompts to the editable itinerary.
- Destination-aware general transport guidance.
- Match itinerary activities against attraction records and show booking/entry reminders.
- Seven-item, destination-scoped checklist saved locally.
- Buffer-time and last-mile reminders.
- Responsive layout.

## Accuracy safeguards
- This is not live routing or transit integration.
- No travel time, fare, service status, booking availability, or booking requirement is invented.
- A booking reminder is a prompt to check official venue information, not a claim that booking is mandatory.
- Xinjiang is treated as a large region requiring intercity and regional planning.
