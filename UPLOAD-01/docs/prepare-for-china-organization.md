# Prepare for China organization

Prepare for China is organized into five collapsible subcategories:
- Entry & arrival: entry/document and before-arrival guides
- Payments: Alipay tutorial
- Connectivity & apps: app directory and setup
- Getting around & daily life: transport, ride-hailing, delivery, dining, hotel and safety guides grouped into topic accordions
- Before-you-go checklist: persistent checklist

The guide cards are grouped by topic and collapsed by default to reduce visual overload. Existing guide content and interactions are retained.
