# On Your Trip organization

The On Your Trip category now presents the emergency information and phrasebook as focused collapsible groups:
- Emergency & safety: emergency numbers and safety guide.
- Getting around: travel essentials guide entry point.
- Phrasebook groups: Getting help & emergencies, Everyday communication, Transport & directions, Shopping & payments.

Phrase cards retain English meaning, Chinese, Pinyin, and the Copy Chinese action. The phrase search remains available and hides empty groups while filtering.
